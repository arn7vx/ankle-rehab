Final gate

Return to game

A match should not be the first time the ankle sees maximal speed, hard braking, reactive cutting, fatigue or ball pressure. The working return gate is: full-speed futsal movements tolerated, at least two full training exposures documented, no meaningful next-day flare, no giving-way, and confidence high enough that you are not protecting the ankle.

When those are met, the app marks you as a return-to-game candidate in the ChatGPT report — not “medically cleared.” The first match exposure should still be conservative rather than jumping straight into unrestricted minutes.

        Safety boundary. This tracker helps organise progressive rehabilitation; it does not diagnose ligament grade, syndesmotic injury, osteochondral injury or mechanical instability. With combined medial + lateral injury, persistent significant medial pain, pain above the ankle with rotation, recurrent giving-way, locking/catching, marked swelling, numbness/colour change, or inability to tolerate basic hopping this far after injury are reasons to seek in-person assessment when possible.
      

    


    

      Framework: pain, ankle impairment, athlete perception, sensorimotor control and sport performance (PAASS), combined with progressive loading and balance/neuromuscular rehabilitation principles. Working symmetry targets in this app are practical rehab guides rather than validated medical clearance thresholds.
      


      References: Smith et al., BJSM 2021, PAASS return-to-sport consensus; Martin et al., JOSPT 2021 ankle stability/movement coordination CPG.
